# Operator Skill: Creative Intelligence

## Purpose
Structure provided creative examples or campaign notes into draft insights.

## When To Use
Use when a maintainer provides ad examples, landing page notes, creative directions, or swipe-file observations.

## Required Input
- Creative examples or descriptions
- Audience or campaign goal
- Evaluation criteria
- Optional constraints

## Safe Output
- Pattern summary
- Creative hypotheses
- Message angles
- Review questions

## Permission Class
draft-only

## Constraints
- Do not scrape websites or external platforms.
- Do not launch campaigns.
- Do not change ad spend.
- Do not copy protected creative assets.

## Verification Checklist
- Separate observed patterns from hypotheses.
- Flag missing source context.
- Keep suggestions generic and reviewable.

## Next Action
Ask for review before using the draft in campaign planning.

## Safety Boundary
This skill drafts, summarizes, or structures information only.
It does not send messages, update systems, publish content, spend money, or perform external writes.
