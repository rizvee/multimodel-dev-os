# Operator Skill: Content Brief

## Purpose
Create a draft content brief from provided topic, audience, and positioning notes.

## When To Use
Use when public-facing content needs a structured brief before drafting.

## Required Input
- Topic
- Audience
- Goal
- Source notes
- Constraints or claims to avoid

## Safe Output
- Draft brief
- Search or audience intent
- Outline
- Acceptance criteria

## Permission Class
draft-only

## Constraints
- Do not publish content.
- Do not claim unreleased behavior.
- Do not include private business data.
- Keep recommendations reviewable.

## Verification Checklist
- Audience, goal, outline, and acceptance criteria are present.
- Claims are sourced from provided context.
- Open questions are listed.

## Next Action
Ask for editorial review before drafting or publishing.

## Safety Boundary
This skill drafts, summarizes, or structures information only.
It does not send messages, update systems, publish content, spend money, or perform external writes.
