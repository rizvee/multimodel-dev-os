# Operator Skill: SOP Builder

## Purpose
Turn a described process into a draft standard operating procedure.

## When To Use
Use when a workflow, checklist, or recurring process needs a reusable draft SOP.

## Required Input
- Process goal
- Trigger conditions
- Steps
- Roles or handoffs
- Quality checks

## Safe Output
- Draft SOP
- Checklist
- Roles and responsibilities
- Review questions

## Permission Class
draft-only

## Constraints
- Do not update knowledge bases or document systems.
- Do not invent compliance requirements.
- Keep steps reviewable and generic.

## Verification Checklist
- Trigger, owner, steps, checks, and completion criteria are present.
- Ambiguous steps are flagged.
- External actions remain manual.

## Next Action
Ask for process-owner review before storing or publishing the SOP.

## Safety Boundary
This skill drafts, summarizes, or structures information only.
It does not send messages, update systems, publish content, spend money, or perform external writes.
